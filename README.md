# AfriBiz Insights: An AI Research Intelligence Platform for African Business Management

> A subscription platform that aggregates, summarizes, and translates open-access African business and management research into actionable briefings for managers, investors, and policymakers operating in African markets.

An interactive prototype generated by [Seedlabs](https://seedlabs.tech) from peer-reviewed research.

## What is Seedlabs?

[Seedlabs](https://seedlabs.tech) turns recent peer-reviewed research into concrete, commercially plausible startup ideas. Every idea is derived from published papers, scored by an AI judge against a fixed rubric (evidence, market, novelty, feasibility, wedge, simplicity), and shipped with a live interactive prototype like this one. [Browse the full catalog](https://seedlabs.tech/ideas).

## Using this prototype

- Open the **[live demo](https://afribiz-insights-an-ai-ab33.seedlabs.tech)** and click through it like an end user — the screens, flows and interactions are the point.
- It is AI-generated and front-end only: every value is illustrative, nothing is persisted, and no real backend is connected. Feel the product; don't run it in production.
- The reasoning behind every screen — which paper claims what, why this wedge, who the first users are — is on the **[idea page](https://seedlabs.tech/idea/afribiz-insights-an-ai-research-intelligence-pla-dfd68fd)**.
